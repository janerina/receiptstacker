ReceiptStacker is a mobile application designed to help users organize receipts, track spending, and manage budgets. The App primarily operates on the user’s device and does not operate its own cloud sync service. Any data leaving your device occurs only when you take an explicit action (for example, exporting/sharing a file, selecting a file to restore, or sending an email to support). This Privacy Policy outlines our strict commitment to privacy, transparency, and user control.

ReceiptStacker collects only the information you voluntarily enter into the App, including:

- Receipt images or text

- Any additional information you manually input  

**The App does NOT automatically collect:**

- Personal identifiers (unless you enter them yourself)  
- Location data  
- Contact information

- Analytics or usage data
