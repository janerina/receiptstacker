# User Manual — About Section (ReceiptStacker)

_Last Updated: 2026-01-31_

This manual explains every item under **Settings → About** in ReceiptStacker, what it does, and how to use it.

---

## 1) Help

### What it is
The **Help** item opens an in-app Help panel. It is designed to give you quick guidance and a direct way to contact support.

### How to open
1. Go to **Settings**.
2. Scroll to the **ABOUT** section.
3. Tap **Help**.

### What you will see
- A modal panel titled **Help**.
- Guidance text (tips for troubleshooting and best practices).
- A **Support** section showing the support email.

### Support email
- ReceiptStacker shows the support email: **support@receiptstacker.com**.
- Tap the email address to open your device’s email app using a `mailto:` link.
- Your email client will open a new draft addressed to support.

#### If the email link doesn’t open
- Ensure you have an email app configured on the device.
- Try again after setting a default email app.
- If your device blocks the action, you may need to allow the email app to handle `mailto:` links.

### Data and privacy notes
- ReceiptStacker does not automatically send your receipt data.
- The email you send is fully controlled by you.
- Only the content you type or attach will be transmitted.

## 2) Privacy Policy

### What it is
The **Privacy Policy** item opens an in-app panel that displays ReceiptStacker’s privacy policy.

### How to open
1. Go to **Settings**.
2. Scroll to **ABOUT**.
3. Tap **Privacy Policy**.

### What you will see
- A modal panel titled **Privacy Policy**.
- The privacy policy text.

### Why it matters
The privacy policy explains:
- What data ReceiptStacker stores (typically local/on-device data you enter)
- What data ReceiptStacker does not collect automatically (for example, analytics, tracking, advertising identifiers)
- How export/backup features work and what happens when you share files

## 3) Terms of Service

### What it is
The **Terms of Service** item opens an in-app panel that displays ReceiptStacker’s Terms of Service.

### How to open
1. Go to **Settings**.
2. Scroll to **ABOUT**.
3. Tap **Terms of Service**.

### What you will see
- A modal panel titled **Terms of Service**.
- The terms text.

### Common topics covered
Depending on your configured Terms of Service, it typically includes:
- Acceptable use
- Limitation of liability
- Local-only nature of the app
- User responsibility for backups and device security

---

## 4) App Version

### What it is
**App Version** shows the current installed version of ReceiptStacker.

### How to use
- Go to **Settings → ABOUT → App Version**.
- The right side shows the version label (example: `v0.0.1`).
- Tapping it also shows an alert with the same version.

### When to use it
- Include the app version when reporting a bug.
- Use it to confirm you installed the latest build.

## Troubleshooting & FAQ (About Section)

### I tapped Help/Privacy/Terms but nothing happened
- If the modal does not open, restart the app and try again.

### I tapped the support email and nothing happened
- Configure an email app on your device.
- Ensure the device can handle `mailto:` links.

### Does About collect analytics?
- No analytics or tracking is intended to run from the About section.

## Tips for support requests
When emailing support, include:
- Device model (example: Pixel 7)
- OS version (example: Android 15)
- ReceiptStacker app version (from **About → App Version**)
- Steps to reproduce the issue
- Screenshots (optional)
