# Quick Start Guide — About Section (ReceiptStacker)

_Last Updated: 2026-01-31_

This guide provides quick actions for every item under **Settings → About**.

---

## Help (Quick Actions)

- Open: **Settings → ABOUT → Help**
- Contact support fast: Tap **support@receiptstacker.com** → send email from your mail app
- Use when: Something isn’t working, you need help, or you want to report a bug

Best practice when contacting support:
- Include your **App Version** from the About section
- Include device model + OS version

---

## Privacy Policy (Quick Actions)

- Open: **Settings → ABOUT → Privacy Policy**
- Use when: You want to understand what data is stored locally, how backups/exports work, and what is (not) collected

---

## Terms of Service (Quick Actions)

- Open: **Settings → ABOUT → Terms of Service**
- Use when: You want to review usage rules and limitation of liability

---

## App Version (Quick Actions)

- View version: **Settings → ABOUT → App Version** (shown on the right)
- Copy into a support message: Type it into your email when reporting issues
- Confirm a build: Compare the shown version after installing an update
